# v0.9.9 explanation and what-if audit

## Result

Plans and outlooks keep a versioned 24-hour snapshot of facts, suggestions,
inputs, and compact result state. The model selects known fact and suggestion
IDs; Hob renders stored text and discards unknown IDs.

What-if queries clone current tasks, apply bounded temporary duration, split,
budget, energy, or work-window inputs, and rerun feasibility. Saved tasks,
settings, Calendar data, and adopted plans stay unchanged. Day results remain
proposals until adopted.

A separate model check distinguishes temporary questions from durable edits
while analysis context is active. Real mutations invalidate the old snapshot.

## Evidence

- 431 Python tests
- Real-model corpus expanded from 102 to 109 cases
- Analysis eval covering deferred-work explanation, combined what-if, and
  unchanged durable state
- Full lock, syntax, Swift, EventKit, Xcode, and exact-commit CI gates
