# v0.9 capacity and evening-loop audit

Date: 2026-07-11.

## Shipped

- Explicit planning days in setup, settings, configuration, and undo
- Read-only seven-day capacity outlook
- Calendar, break, buffer, dependency, deadline, fixed-work, and remaining-effort
  accounting
- Natural overload, fit, deadline, and weekly-budget questions
- Plan-aware evening recap without inferred completion
- First-adoption coaching
- Upgrade preservation for older all-days profiles

Outlooks produce risks and capacity facts. They do not move tasks, adopt plans,
or write Calendar data.

## Implementation findings

- Older profiles needed all-days compatibility to preserve weekend capacity.
- Named what-if boundaries needed typed dates and literal time budgets.
- Fixed work needed explicit conflict language.
- `Never mind` after a recent mutation was initially captured as a task. v0.9.1
  added model-owned retraction and restored the live action through normal undo.
- Status lacked enough plan-adoption evidence. Aggregate run, session, and nudge
  counts were added without task text.
